About This Bag

The Winged Tote is Softline Theory's signature silhouette — the one that started it all. The flared winged edges aren't just decorative; they create a natural opening that makes finding your essentials effortless, while giving the bag an architectural presence that turns heads without shouting for attention.

Built from heavily pebbled faux leather that ages beautifully, the Winged Tote features a centre divider that separates your main compartment into two organised zones — work and personal, or essentials and extras. The zip-top closure provides security without sacrificing the open, airy feel when you're reaching in. A matching charm hangs from the handle as a quiet signature detail.

This is the bag for women who carry their whole life with them but refuse to look like they're carrying their whole life. The proportions are generous enough for a 14" laptop, a water bottle, and your daily necessities, yet the structured shape means it never looks bulky. In Cream, it's the neutral hero your wardrobe has been waiting for.
