About This Bag

The Lace Bow Tote was born from a simple idea: everyday bags don't have to choose between charming and practical. The structured trapezoidal silhouette holds its shape beautifully, while the delicate lace bow detail adds a touch of soft femininity that makes it feel special without trying too hard.

Crafted from pebbled faux leather with a satisfying tactile finish, this tote transitions seamlessly from a morning coffee run to a dinner date. The magnetic snap closure keeps everything secure, while the inner zip pocket and two slip pockets give your essentials a proper place. Available in six carefully curated colours — from classic Black to dreamy Lavender — there's a shade for every mood.

The Mini size is perfect for those lighter days when you just need your phone, cardholder, and a lip gloss. Both sizes feature the same thoughtful proportions and signature bow detail that makes this our most versatile design.
