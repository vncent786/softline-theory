About This Bag

The Woven Mini Tote Casual is the relaxed sibling in the family — a softly curved baguette-style shoulder bag with the same intricate woven leather texture, but a slouchier, more easygoing personality. A single braided handle drapes comfortably over the shoulder for hands-free ease.

Where the structured mini tote holds its line, the Casual gives a little. It's the bag you grab for market mornings, long lunches, and the in-between moments that make up most of life. Soft enough to mould against your side, structured enough to keep its shape.

Eight colours span the spectrum — from grounding Black, Brown, and Blue to cheerful Yellow, fresh Green, warm Beige, crisp White, and bold Red — so you can pick the personality that matches your day.
